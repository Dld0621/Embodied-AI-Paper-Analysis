Selection contract

Small enough to trust. Broad enough to navigate.

A paper enters the core map only when its year, venue, embodied relevance, and official acceptance source are explicit.

  1. 01
    Rolling five-year window

    Conference years 2022 through 2026, inclusive.

  2. 02
    Formal acceptance

    Main conference or official conference track—not workshop, withdrawn, under review, or arXiv-only.

  3. 03
    One paper, one venue

    No ambiguous labels such as RSS/CoRL/ICRA and no duplicate title variants.

  4. 04
    Official provenance

    Every row carries a proceedings, conference, publisher, or accepted OpenReview source.
