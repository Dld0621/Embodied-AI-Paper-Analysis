Read the full methodology ↗
  1. 01
    Rolling five-year window

    Conference years 2022 through 2026, inclusive.

  2. 02
    Reproducible discovery

    Ten fixed venues are searched through conference-indexed metadata with the query “robot”.

  3. 03
    Deterministic admission

    Title taxonomy, medical exclusions, normalized-title deduplication, and one primary direction per paper.

  4. 04
    Tiered provenance

    Every row links a paper plus an official, publisher, or bibliographic source whose tier is shown honestly.
